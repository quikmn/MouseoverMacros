EverlookDB Macros: your macros, sorted, for WoW Forever. It used to be called MouseoverMacros.

### A macro editor next to all your macros
Every macro you have, account and character together, in one list that shows what each one casts. Click one and edit it right beside the list: name, icon, a live check of every spell name and the 255 count, Insert spell from your spellbook (with a rank), condition buttons, Save and Undo. Drag a macro straight onto your action bars, or click Put on bar.

### Mouseover macros in a few clicks
Pick a spell for the friend under your mouse, one for the enemy, or both, with the rank you want. You see the macro before you make it. Create saves it and puts it on your cursor, ready to drop on a bar. It never casts on you.

### A mouseover set for every class
Every class has its own set: the spells worth a mouseover macro from level 1 to 60, in every spec. Heals, shields, dispels, buffs and resurrections go on the friend under your mouse, else your friendly target. DoTs, curses, interrupts, crowd control and taunts go on the enemy under your mouse, else your target. Holy Shock, Dispel Magic and Penance work on either. None of them ever falls back to casting on you. Self-only spells, stances, forms, auras, totems and ground spells are left out.

Switch each macro on or off, then "Make my whole mouseover set". Each macro uses the spell's name in your game's language and always casts your highest rank.

### Set up on first login
The first time you log in on a character, it makes the mouseover macros for the spells that character knows and says so in one chat line. It uses character macro slots only, always leaves 3 free for your own, and names what didn't fit. When you learn a new spell, at a trainer or from a talent, its macro is made too. A macro you delete is never made again, and your own macros are never touched. Switch it off under Options.

### Uses all your slots
New macros go to a character slot, else to an account slot, and the window tells you which. When everything is full it says so plainly.

### Backups and sharing
Delete is one click, and Undo puts it back. The last 10 versions of each macro are kept, the last 100 deleted macros can be restored, and sets export and import as text. Anything you ask for in combat waits for the fight to end, and the window says so.

### The ring
Hold the middle mouse button (or any key), flick toward a slice and let go: your best food, drink, potions and bandage, your buffs, your professions, fishing with your pole and lures, your hearthstone. Sub-rings, drag and drop editing, and it works in combat through the game's secure buttons.

### Quick picks
Common macros ready to make: both trinkets, focus or skull on mouseover, target and attack, assist focus, ready check, and for priests Devouring Plague / Flash Heal rank 1, Devouring Plague / Power Infusion and more.

### Using it
/mom (or /emacros) opens the window, and /macro opens it too (you can give /macro back to Blizzard). /mom help lists the commands.
